                    How these are worked out

                    Prices are TCGplayer's, through Scryfall and MTGJSON, in US dollars converted to yours. Each is the median of its last 3 days, so one odd sale doesn't make a card rise or fall. Prices aren't split by condition, so a card that rarely sells can swing with which copies sold.
